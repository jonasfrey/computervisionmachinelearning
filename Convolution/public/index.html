What are we computing?

  1. Take a patch. Place the kernel over the image, including any padded zeros.
  2. Multiply and add. Pair each pixel with its weight. Sum all products into one output cell.
  3. Slide and repeat. Move by the stride. At the end of a row, return left and move down.
